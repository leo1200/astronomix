My vision is field-level inference on the Cas A supernova remmant.
Cas A is uniquely suited because Chandra has observed it over the
last ~30 years such that we have true dynamics on which we can e.g.
do multiple shooting. The goal would be to sample initial fields
at e.g. 150 years that produce the correct subsequent evolution
and in addition some physical parameters governing the evolution in 
this phase. Ideally one would do proper Bayesian modeling - surely
there are ambiguities.
